How it works

Four steps from stand to keys.

01

Set up the project

Add the stand, budget in USD and target handover date. YaVaKa builds the full Zimbabwean stage plan and budget split automatically.

02

Invite your build manager

Your brother, a builder or a professional project manager gets a workspace login. You keep the owner view, they post progress and expenses.

03

Fund and approve

Send tranches when milestones are met. Each expense is tagged to a stage and needs a receipt before it leaves the balance.

04

Handover with the full file

Certificate of Occupation, warranties, snag list and every receipt — exported as a complete project record when you finally get the keys.
