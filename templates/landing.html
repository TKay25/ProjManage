Independent build management · Zimbabwe

You can't be on site.
We can.

YaVaKa manages your Zimbabwe build while your contractor does the work — vetting the builder, approving every payment and settling the arguments. Everything is logged as it happens, so you can watch it from anywhere.

Walls up.
Evidence logged.

Every stage is inspected against the approved drawings before the next payment is released. You see the stage, the photographs and the approval — not a phone call asking you to trust it.

The kitchen you agreed.
Not a cheaper version.

Tiles, units and fittings are matched against the signed bill of quantities as they arrive on site. If the specification changes, you approve the variation before the order is placed — not after.

The work you'd never see.
Written down.

First-fix wiring, plumbing and waterproofing are inspected and photographed before the walls close. The evidence stays with the stage it belongs to — not in a phone that gets lost.

The honest part

Building from abroad isn't a distance problem. It's a trust problem.

Almost every diaspora build stalls the same way. The money is real, the intentions are good, and nobody with authority is standing on the site.

None of that is a paperwork problem. It's a presence problem. Somebody has to stand on that site, hold the money and take the calls. That somebody is us.

  1. 01

    The money finished. The house didn't.

    Tranche two cleared, the site went quiet, and no one could say exactly what the money touched.

  2. 02

    The photos always look good.

    A camera can make a weak mix look like progress. Nobody was checking the specification, the quantities or the curing.

  3. 03

    Your relative means well. He isn't an engineer.

    Family can watch a site. Reading a bill of quantities, testing concrete and holding retention is a different job.

  4. 04

    Then comes the diaspora price.

    The moment a contractor hears the client is abroad, the same cement costs a third more. Nobody is negotiating for you.

  5. 05

    The argument you cannot win.

    Delays, "extras", a threat to down tools. You argue on WhatsApp across time zones, and he can simply wait you out.

  6. 06

    Approvals nobody warned you about.

    Title, cession, council plans, EMA, ZINWA, ZESA. One missed gate stops the entire build while it is explained to you.

How it works

From first land check to the keys in your hand.

01

Give the build a home

Add the stand, USD budget and target handover date. YaVaKa creates a structured stage plan and starting budget split for your project.

02

Put the right people on the record

Invite your own manager—or choose YaVaKa to manage the build. Keep responsibilities, progress and project updates visible to the people involved.

03

Make every movement explainable

Record funds as they arrive and project payments as they are made. Link expenses to the right stage, add receipts and see the available balance update.

04

Reach handover with the history intact

Bring the completion records, warranties, snag list and receipts together, so the story of the build is still there when the keys are handed over.

Why we exist

Zimbabwe is full of good builders and unfinished houses.

The money is usually real and the intentions are usually good. What is missing is someone accountable standing on the site, in writing. YaVaKa was built for that gap.

Our vision

A Zimbabwe where distance never decides the quality of a build.

Any Zimbabwean abroad should be able to build at home with the same confidence they would have standing on the site themselves, without flying back or hoping.

Our mission

Independent oversight, recorded evidence, one accountable team.

We supervise or deliver the build, approve every payment against proof, and keep one record that the client, the contractor and the family can all read.

Our objectives

  • No payment released without a receipt or an approved stage.
  • Progress visible to the client from anywhere, on any device.
  • Every build closes with a complete, handover-ready file.
  • Build managers run more projects without more paperwork.
  • Suppliers and contractors held to a documented standard.
