Why YaVaKa exists

You may be miles away. Your project record shouldn't be.

Building from London, Johannesburg or Dubai should not mean piecing together updates from chats, calls and scattered receipts. YaVaKa gives you and your on-the-ground team one shared place to see what happened, what it cost and what needs attention next.

{{ icon('wallet', 22) }}

Know where every dollar went.

Record funds received, project payments and expenses in one USD ledger. Link costs to a build stage and attach the receipt, so the balance has a clear story—not a trail of screenshots.

{{ icon('shield', 22) }}

Know what's next—and what must happen first.

Follow a 19-stage Zimbabwe build sequence, from land and title checks to council approvals, services and handover. See the relevant authority at each statutory gate.

{{ icon('folder', 22) }}

Keep the proof with the project.

Plans, approvals, invoices, receipts, site photos and signed agreements stay filed against the build and the stage they belong to—not buried in someone's phone gallery.

{{ icon('message', 22) }}

Turn updates into a record you can follow.

Your team can share progress through YaVaKa's workspace and WhatsApp tools. Important project details stay connected to the same build record.

How it works

From first land check to the keys in your hand.

01

Give the build a home

Add the stand, USD budget and target handover date. YaVaKa creates a structured stage plan and starting budget split for your project.

02

Put the right people on the record

Invite your own manager—or choose YaVaKa to manage the build. Keep responsibilities, progress and project updates visible to the people involved.

03

Make every movement explainable

Record funds as they arrive and project payments as they are made. Link expenses to the right stage, add receipts and see the available balance update.

04

Reach handover with the history intact

Bring the completion records, warranties, snag list and receipts together, so the story of the build is still there when the keys are handed over.
