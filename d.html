Scope of works

Design, council approval support, construction and handover of a three-bedroom house (approximately 180m²) on Stand 1421, Greendale, Harare, including external works and connection applications.

Payment schedule

MilestoneAmount
Signing and site mobilisation $17,000.00
Foundation, slab and superstructure $25,500.00
Roof, services and finishes $25,500.00
Practical completion and handover $17,000.00
Total scheduled $85,000.00

Terms

1. The contractor will build in accordance with the council-approved drawings and the specifications attached to this agreement. 2. The contract price is USD 85,000.00, payable against the milestones set out in the payment schedule. 3. Client funds are held in the project account and released per completed and inspected milestone. 4. Any variation must be agreed in writing before the work is carried out and will be priced separately. 5. The contractor is responsible for site safety and for statutory inspections at foundation, roofing and completion stages. 6. Practical completion is targeted for 15 December 2026, subject to council approvals and client-funded materials arriving on time. 7. The contractor holds 5% retention from each payment until the snag list is closed, then releases it within 14 days. 8. Either party may refer a dispute to mediation, then arbitration under the Arbitration Act [Chapter 7:15] of Zimbabwe. 9. This agreement is governed by the laws of Zimbabwe.
